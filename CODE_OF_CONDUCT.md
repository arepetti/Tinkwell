# Code of Conduct

Tinkwell follows the **[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org) v2.1**.
The full text is available at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>; this
page is a one-page summary of what it means for this repository.

## Our pledge

We — maintainers and contributors — pledge to make participation in the Tinkwell project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Behaviour that contributes to a positive environment includes:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Accepting responsibility, apologising to those affected by mistakes, and learning from the experience.
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community.

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing other people's private information (physical address, email address, etc.) without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

The project maintainer (see [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md)) is responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of acceptable behaviour, and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behaviour that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

The maintainer has the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and any official communication channel linked from this repository — and also when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

Behaviour in the spaces of the **sibling repositories** listed under *Related repositories* in [README.md](README.md) is governed by the Codes of Conduct of those repositories.

## Reporting

Please report unacceptable behaviour privately through GitHub's repository security advisory form:

- <https://github.com/arepetti/Tinkwell/security/advisories/new>

This is the same private channel used for security reports (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)); using it for Code of Conduct reports is deliberate — it is the only private, authenticated reporting channel the project offers.
Clearly label the report as a **Code of Conduct** concern in the title so it is routed correctly.

If GitHub's private reporting is unavailable for any reason, contact `@arepetti` on GitHub through the public profile links there.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly on a best-effort basis, consistent with the solo-maintained nature of the project.
The maintainer is obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

## Enforcement guidelines

The maintainer will follow the Contributor Covenant's enforcement ladder (Correction → Warning → Temporary ban → Permanent ban) as described in §4 of the canonical text, adapted proportionally to the infraction.
